# Teavy 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日

Teavy 不收集、不存储、也不向开发者或任何第三方发送你的个人数据。

- **连接信息**：你保存的连接（名称、地址、端口、用户名、SSH 设置）存在你的设备上，并经你自己的 iCloud 账户在你的设备间同步。开发者无法访问这些数据。
- **密码和密钥**：VNC 密码、Mac 账户密码、SSH 密码和 SSH 私钥存在 iOS 钥匙串中，开启 iCloud 钥匙串时随之同步。开发者无法访问。
- **远程画面和输入**：Teavy 直接连接你指定的电脑，画面、键盘输入和剪贴板只在你的设备和那台电脑之间传输，不经过开发者的服务器。
- **本地网络**：Teavy 请求本地网络权限，只用于发现和连接局域网中的电脑。
- **剪贴板**：只有在你点“发送剪贴板”时，Teavy 才读取剪贴板并发送给你正在连接的电脑；远程电脑复制的文字会写入本机剪贴板。
- Teavy 不含广告、分析或跟踪代码。

本政策如有变更，会更新在本页并修改生效日期。如有疑问，请联系 [dev@xshowee.com](mailto:dev@xshowee.com)。

# Teavy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Teavy doesn't collect, store, or send your personal data to the developer or any third party.

- **Connections**: The connections you save (name, address, port, username, SSH settings) are stored on your device and synced across your devices through your own iCloud account. The developer can't access them.
- **Passwords and keys**: VNC passwords, Mac account passwords, SSH passwords, and your SSH private key are stored in the iOS keychain and sync with iCloud Keychain if it's turned on. The developer can't access them.
- **Remote screen and input**: Teavy connects directly to the computers you specify. The screen, keyboard input, and clipboard travel only between your device and that computer, never through the developer's servers.
- **Local network**: Teavy asks for local network access only to find and connect to computers on your network.
- **Clipboard**: Teavy reads your clipboard only when you tap Send Clipboard, and sends it to the computer you're connected to. Text copied on the remote computer is placed on your device's clipboard.
- Teavy contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking code.

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Contact [dev@xshowee.com](mailto:dev@xshowee.com).
